    Your build belongs in the picture

    Share a screenshot in our Discord and, once a moderator has checked it, it can appear here.

    1. Post your screenshot in #show-off on Discord, with a line about what it is.
    2. Press Submit to gallery on the reply under your post. Only you can submit your own post, and submitting means you're happy for it to be shown on this page.
    3. Staff approve every shot before it goes up. Shots showing coordinates, chat or other players' nameplates cannot be approved.
    4. After submitting, use the buttons to choose your linked Minecraft name or No name (anonymous). Link Minecraft before submitting if you want named credit.
